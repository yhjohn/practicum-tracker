CLIENT OVERVIEW & RIGHTS

Counseling is a professional relationship that provides a safe and confidential space to explore and address emotional, psychological, behavioral issues. As your counselor, I am committed to assisting you in addressing the concerns you bring to our sessions. This informed consent form is designed to ensure that you fully understand the counseling process and your rights as a client.

The regular sessions will last approximately 45-60 minutes with the frequency being according to the needs of the client.

You have the right to terminate counseling services at any time. If, in my professional judgment, I believe that I am unable to provide the necessary assistance, or if you request services outside my scope of practice, I may terminate the counseling relationship and provide referrals as appropriate.

CANCELLATION AND SCHEDULING

Our sessions will be held on a regular basis, as discussed and agreed upon. If you need to cancel or reschedule a session, please provide at least 24 hours’ notice.

CONFIDENTIALITY

Counseling sessions are confidential. This means that the content of our sessions is kept private. However, there are legal and ethical exceptions to confidentiality, which include:

  1. Harm to Self or Others: If you pose a danger to yourself or others, I may need to take necessary steps to ensure safety.
  2. Court Orders: In certain legal situations, I may be required to disclose information as mandated by a court order.
  3. Client’s Request: If you request that I share specific information with another individual or entity, I will do so only with your written consent.
  4. Consultation: For professional development and supervision purposes, I may discuss our sessions with my supervisor. In these instances, your identity will remain protected.
